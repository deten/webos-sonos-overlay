Sonos Volume Overlay

This is in testing. Use at your own risk. It requires a rooted TV and patches a system file belonging to the compositor.

When a TV is set to external ARC sound output, webOS hides its own volume display and hands control to HDMI-CEC. The remote still changes the volume, but no number appears on screen and the TV and the soundbar drift apart.

This restores the on-screen indicator and keeps the soundbar locked to the TV volume. After installing, open the app once to pick your Sonos player.

Tested on webOS 6.4.0. It runs on any release and tells you whether yours has been tested.
